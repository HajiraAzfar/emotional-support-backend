Worry time helps when worries spread across the whole day or keep you awake at night. Choose a fixed 15 to 20 minutes each day, not close to bedtime. When a worry comes at another time, write it down in a few words and tell yourself you will think about it in worry time. When worry time comes, go through the list: some worries will have faded, and for the rest decide whether there is something you can do. If there is, plan one small step; if not, practise setting it aside until tomorrow.
