Low mood makes us do less, and doing less makes the mood lower. Behavioural activation breaks this loop by acting first, before motivation arrives. Pick one small, specific activity: a short walk, a shower, calling a friend, or one task you have been putting off. Make it small enough to feel almost too easy, decide when you will do it, then do it and notice how you feel afterwards. Mix activities that bring pleasure with ones that bring a sense of achievement, and build up slowly.
