AI Chat lets you talk with Reframe, the app's support companion. Open it from Home under quick tools. Type a message, or tap the microphone to speak. Exit takes you back to Home, and Get help in the header opens support resources at any time. Under the message box, New chat starts a fresh chat and End & save closes this one; either way the chat stays saved. Earlier chats are in History under the Chats filter. Reframe is a support companion, not a therapist.
