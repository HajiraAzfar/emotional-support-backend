The Exposure Journal (Facing something) helps you face a fear in small steps. Open it from Home (Quick actions, Exposure) or the Journal tab. First build a ladder: name the fear and add up to 10 steps, each with how anxious you expect to feel; you can sort them from easiest to hardest. Then pick a step: plan it, note how it goes during, and review afterwards. There is also a Quick option, and your history and insights show how your anxiety changes over time.
