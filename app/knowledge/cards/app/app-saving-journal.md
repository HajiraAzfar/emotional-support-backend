The Saving Journal is for good moments, so you notice and keep them. Open it from Home (Quick actions, Saving). Capture a moment with Quick or Full. Its home shows your recent moments, a memory lane of older ones, and a calendar heatmap with your streak. You can look back through your history and insights at any time.
