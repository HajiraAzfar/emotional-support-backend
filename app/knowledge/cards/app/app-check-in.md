A check-in records how you feel right now. Open it from Home (Quick actions, Check-in) or from the Journal tab. You can do up to three a day: morning, afternoon and evening. First pick your mood, then choose what is behind it from the chips (you can search in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, or add your own), add a note if you like, and finish. Once a week it also asks a few short questions about mood and worry. If a check-in shows high stress or very low mood, it may offer the Thought Journal.
