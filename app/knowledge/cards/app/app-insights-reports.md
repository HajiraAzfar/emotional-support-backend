The Insights tab shows charts of your mood and trends, a mood calendar, and a heatmap of all your journals. For a PDF, go to Insights (or Check-ins) and open Reports: daily, weekly and monthly reports appear once that day, week or month is over, and each one downloads to your phone's Downloads folder. Keep a copy, in Insights, saves a PDF of your insights.
