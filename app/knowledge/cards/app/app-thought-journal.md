The Thought Journal is the Thoughts tab (also on Home under Quick actions). Choose Quick or Full. Quick asks for the situation, your feelings and how strong they are, the thought, how much you believe it, a more balanced thought, and then you rate again. Full adds thinking patterns, evidence for and against the thought with helper questions, and a next step. You can speak instead of typing on any text step. Your progress saves as you go: Continue where you left off, on its home, brings back a draft for 7 days. AI help appears only if you ask for it.
