When the body is anxious, breathing becomes fast and shallow, which can make the heart race even more. Slow breathing sends the opposite signal. Breathe in gently through the nose for about four counts, then breathe out slowly for about six counts, letting the shoulders drop. Making the out-breath longer than the in-breath is the important part. Keep going for two to five minutes. If counting feels hard, simply follow the breath with your attention and let each out-breath be a little slower.
