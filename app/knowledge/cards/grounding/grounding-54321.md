Grounding brings attention back to the present when strong feelings or panic take over. Slowly name five things you can see, four things you can touch, three things you can hear, two things you can smell and one thing you can taste. Take your time with each one and describe it to yourself in detail. Pressing your feet into the floor and noticing the chair under you also helps. It does not remove the feeling, but it gives the mind something steady to hold while the wave passes.
