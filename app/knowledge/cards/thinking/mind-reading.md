Mind reading is a thinking trap where we feel sure we know what someone else is thinking about us, usually something bad, without them saying it. A late reply becomes 'she must be angry with me'. Notice the jump from what happened to what you guessed. Ask: what do I actually know, and what am I assuming? What else could explain it? If it matters, the only way to know is to ask them. Holding the guess lightly often eases the hurt it causes.
