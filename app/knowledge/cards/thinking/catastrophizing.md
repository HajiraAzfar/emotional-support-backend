Catastrophizing is a thinking trap where the mind jumps to the worst possible outcome and treats it as likely. It often starts with 'what if' or 'it will be a disaster'. Ask three questions: what is the worst that could happen, what is the best, and what is most likely? Then ask: if the worst did happen, how would I cope, and who could help? Thinking through the most likely outcome and a coping plan usually shrinks the fear to a size that can be handled.
