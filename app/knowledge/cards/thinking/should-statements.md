'Should' statements are rigid rules we hold ourselves or others to: 'I should never make mistakes', 'I must always be strong'. When the rule is broken, the result is guilt, shame or anger. Notice the words should, must and have to. Ask where the rule came from and whether it is realistic for any person. Try turning it into a preference: 'I would like to do well, and if I make a mistake I can learn from it.' This keeps the value without the harshness.
