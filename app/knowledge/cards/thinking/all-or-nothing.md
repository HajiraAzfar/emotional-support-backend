All-or-nothing thinking sees things in two boxes only: perfect or a total failure, good or useless. One mistake can make a whole day, or a whole self, feel worthless. Look for the space in between. Ask: on a scale from 0 to 100, where does this really sit? What went partly right? Would I judge a friend this harshly for the same thing? Swapping 'always' and 'never' for words like 'this time' or 'sometimes' usually gives a fairer picture.
