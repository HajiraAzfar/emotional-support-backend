A thought record slows a painful thought down so it can be checked instead of obeyed. Write down the situation (where, when, who), the feeling and how strong it is from 0 to 100, and the exact thought that came with it. Then list the facts that support the thought and the facts that don't. Last, write a more balanced thought that fits all the facts, and rate the feeling again. The aim is not a happy thought, only a fairer one. Even a small drop in the rating counts.
