When many problems pile up, everything can feel stuck at once. Structured problem solving takes one problem at a time. Write the problem down in one clear sentence. List every possible solution without judging them yet. Then weigh the pros and cons of each, choose one that seems workable and break it into small steps. Try it, and afterwards look at what happened. If it did not work, that is information, not failure: go back to the list and try another option.
