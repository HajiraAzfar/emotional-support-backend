When strong thoughts and feelings carry you away, grounding helps you get back to the present. First notice what you are thinking and feeling, without judging it. Then slow down and connect with your body: press your feet into the floor, straighten your back, press your hands together, and breathe slowly. Then refocus on the world around you: notice what you can see and hear, where you are and what you are doing. It can take a few seconds or a few minutes, and you can do it many times a day.
