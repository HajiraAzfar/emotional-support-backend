When we are stressed, the voice in our head can become harsh and unkind. Notice when you are being hard on yourself. Then speak to yourself as you would to a good friend who was struggling: 'This is really hard, and I am doing my best.' A kind gesture can help too, like placing a hand on your heart. Small acts of kindness towards others, even a kind word, can also help you feel better.
