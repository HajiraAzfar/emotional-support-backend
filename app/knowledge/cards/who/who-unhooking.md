Difficult thoughts and feelings can hook us and pull us away from what matters. To unhook, first notice that a thought or feeling has hooked you. Then name it, for example 'I am noticing the thought that I am a failure' or 'Here is anger'. Naming it creates a little space: a thought is something your mind is saying, not a fact or an order. Then refocus your attention on what you are doing right now and give it your full attention.
