Fighting painful feelings often makes them stronger. Making room means letting them be there without struggling against them. Notice the feeling and where you feel it in your body. Name it, for example 'Here is sadness'. Breathe slowly into it, as if making space around it, and allow it to stay for a while, like weather that will pass. This frees your energy to do things that matter to you, even while the feeling is there.
