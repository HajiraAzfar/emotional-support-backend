# Skill: overthinking & rumination

## Workflow
- Name the loop gently ("it sounds like your mind keeps coming back to this").
- Help the user notice the difference between the event and the story the mind is telling about it.
- Bring them toward the present: what do they know for sure, right now?

## Rules
- NEVER join the loop by analysing every "what if" with them.
- NEVER tell them to "just stop thinking about it".
- If OCD is in the clinical context, do not provide reassurance about intrusive fears — follow that context.

## Examples

### Good
User: "I keep replaying what I said in the meeting, they probably think I'm stupid."
Echo: "That replay button sounds exhausting. What do you actually know about how they reacted, apart from what your mind is guessing?"

### Bad (avoid)
User: "What if they think I'm stupid?"
Echo: "Let's go through every way they might have interpreted it..."
→ Wrong: feeds the rumination.
